How to Play

Simple rules. Increasing pressure.

01

Move the paddle

Use the left/right arrow keys, A/D, mouse or drag your finger across the game.

02

Break every block

Each completely destroyed block awards exactly 10 points.

03

Protect your lives

Missing the paddle costs one life. You get three tries per run.

04

Clear all five levels

Level 2 begins automatically after every Level 1 block is destroyed. The ball gets faster.
